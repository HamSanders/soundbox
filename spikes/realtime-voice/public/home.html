How a turn works

Each stage streams into the next, so the reply starts before the sentence is finished.

  1. 01ListenBrowser mic, 32 ms frames over a WebSocket
  2. 02DetectSilero VAD; starts a reply after 160 ms of quiet
  3. 03TranscribeNemotron ASR 0.6B, ~20 ms per utterance
  4. 04ThinkQwen3 4B, a persona per face, streamed tokens
  5. 05SpeakVoxCPM2 in your cloned voice, 160 ms chunks
  6. 06ShowTalking face from one photo, audio and frames on one clock
